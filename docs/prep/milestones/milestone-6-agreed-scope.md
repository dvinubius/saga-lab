# Milestone 6 — Agreed scope

Build specification: [GitHub issue #61 — Complete engineering investigation: labelled waits, broker dashboard and transfer links](https://github.com/dvinubius/saga-lab/issues/61).

These decisions refine milestone 6 and narrow it substantially. Traces, the persisted execution history, playback and the outcome summary already explain every scenario; what is missing is labels for the gaps a trace already shows and markers for scenario facts. The [Observability](../saga-lab-03-observability.md) document and the milestone 6 entry in [Milestones](../saga-lab-04-milestones.md) are amended in place to match.

- **Dropped from V1:** Loki and log shipping, broker metrics and the Broker dashboard, application metrics (technical and business/reliability), overview, health and correctness dashboards, and canonical per-transfer log lines. A canonical line would repeat the persisted outcome summary, and without a log store it could not be queried or aggregated. There is no load target or service level, so health metrics explain nothing about a scenario.
- **Logs:** stay structured slog on stderr. The attempt logger adds `trace_id` beside `attempt_id`, so a `docker logs` line leads to its trace. No new log events and no field overhaul.
- **Broker metrics dropped after building them:** a Prometheus-fed "Broker" dashboard linked from Bank B unavailability transfers was built and removed. The only thing it showed that the trace and history do not was the credit command ready in the dedicated queue with no consumer, and reading that asks visitors to understand how the scenario is implemented (Bank B stays online; one dedicated consumer is paused) rather than what it demonstrates. Ready and unacknowledged counts on every other queue stay at zero at one-second sampling, because handling takes milliseconds, and deliveries and redeliveries repeat what the trace shows.
- **Transfer page link:** the existing "Grafana →" trace link becomes "Trace →". Grafana passes the time range to Tempo on a trace-ID lookup, so the link opens on a fixed window from 10 s before `requested_at` to 10 s after the last history entry, or, while pending, to 10 s after the latest moment the resume could still be issued.
- **Trace additions:** spans recorded after the fact with explicit start and end times label the two waits. An "admission wait" span in the Transfer Service runs from `requested_at` to admission, only for transfers that were queued. A "delivery wait" span in the Transfer Service runs from the broker's confirmation of the credit command, when the wait timer starts, to resume issued; it is the wait as the application scheduled it, not a broker measurement. Bank B's dedicated credit processing span gains a `consumer.paused` event once it stops the consumer again, and a credit rejected by the scenario gains a `credit.rejected` event carrying `saga.scenario`, parallel to `fault.injected`.
- **Primary test boundary:** over HTTP against the running stack. `trace_test.go` additionally checks the `credit.rejected` event, refund-redelivery attempts if not already covered, and, for a Bank B unavailability transfer, both wait spans and `consumer.paused`. A link test checks every scenario's trace link and window. The trace view gets a per-scenario eyeball checklist, not browser tests.
