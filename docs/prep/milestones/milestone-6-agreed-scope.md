# Milestone 6 — Agreed scope

Build specification: not yet published.

These decisions refine milestone 6 and narrow it substantially. Traces, the persisted execution history, playback and the outcome summary already explain four of the five scenarios; the only evidence missing is the broker's own view of the delivery wait, plus labels for the gaps a trace already shows. The [Observability](../saga-lab-03-observability.md) document and the milestone 6 entry in [Milestones](../saga-lab-04-milestones.md) are amended in place to match.

- **Dropped from V1:** Loki and log shipping, application metrics (technical and business/reliability), overview, health and correctness dashboards, and canonical per-transfer log lines. A canonical line would repeat the persisted outcome summary, and without a log store it could not be queried or aggregated. There is no load target or service level, so health metrics explain nothing about a scenario.
- **Logs:** stay structured slog on stderr. The attempt logger adds `trace_id` beside `attempt_id`, so a `docker logs` line leads to its trace. No new log events and no field overhaul.
- **Broker metrics:** Prometheus scrapes RabbitMQ's built-in per-object metrics every second, and RabbitMQ's `collect_statistics_interval` drops to one second so a five-second (2.5 s in tests) delivery wait is not lost between statistics updates. Prometheus is provisioned as a Grafana datasource. Images follow the two-week package-age rule.
- **Broker dashboard:** one provisioned "Broker" dashboard. Its top row puts Bank B's normal `CreditFunds` queue beside `CreditFundsDedicated`, each with ready, unacknowledged and consumer counts; a second row shows ready and unacknowledged messages for every queue as context. Because the demonstration slot admits one Bank B unavailability transfer at a time, the dedicated queue's graph over a transfer's window belongs to that transfer.
- **Transfer page links:** the existing "Grafana →" trace link becomes "Trace →". A Bank B unavailability transfer also gets "Broker →", opening the Broker dashboard from 30 s before `requested_at` to 30 s after its last history entry, or to now while pending. The trace link takes the same window only if Tempo honours the range on a trace-ID lookup; otherwise it keeps its current range.
- **Trace additions:** spans recorded after the fact with explicit start and end times label the two waits. An "admission wait" span in the Transfer Service runs from `requested_at` to admission, only for transfers that were queued. A "delivery wait" span in the Transfer Service runs from credit requested to resume issued; it is the wait as the application scheduled it, while the Broker dashboard shows the broker's own view. Bank B's dedicated credit processing span gains a `consumer.paused` event once it stops the consumer again, and a credit rejected by the scenario gains a `credit.rejected` event carrying `saga.scenario`, parallel to `fault.injected`.
- **Primary test boundary:** over HTTP against the running stack. `trace_test.go` additionally checks the `credit.rejected` event, refund-redelivery attempts if not already covered, and, for a Bank B unavailability transfer, both wait spans and `consumer.paused`. One test queries Prometheus over a Bank B unavailability transfer's window: `CreditFundsDedicated` shows at least one ready message with zero consumers, then zero ready. The dashboard and links get a per-scenario eyeball checklist, not browser tests.
